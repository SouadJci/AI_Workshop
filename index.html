The core method
Prompt Formula 🧪
One method. Every task. Consistent results.

The RTCF Method

R
ROLE — Who is the AI in this context?
Give it a persona relevant to your task. This shapes tone, vocabulary, and depth.
"You are a professional communication officer for a youth NGO..."
T
TASK — What exactly do you need?
Specific task = specific output. Vague task = vague output. Be precise.
"Write a 150-word follow-up email to our event sponsors..."
C
CONTEXT — What's the situation?
Most people skip this. Without context, AI invents it — and gets it wrong.
"JCI Tétouan · INNOVACTION competition · 12 teams registered · June 14..."
F
FORMAT — How do you want the output?
Length, tone, language, structure. Without format, AI chooses — usually wrong.
"Professional tone · French · Under 200 words · WhatsApp-friendly"
↺ Iterate — don't restart. Stay in the same chat. Add one follow-up like "Make it shorter" or "Give me 3 versions" — don't start a new conversation.

Your RTCF template

Copy & customize
ROLE: You are [relevant expert role]... TASK: [Exactly what you need — be specific] CONTEXT: [Your organization / project / situation / audience] FORMAT: [Tone · Language · Length · Structure]

Pair exercise · 5 min

Turn to your neighbor. Pick ONE real task you do regularly at JCI. Build a prompt using RTCF. Run it. Then iterate once with a follow-up.
The quality of your output = the quality of your prompt. Every time AI disappoints you — look at your prompt first.

Activity 2 · Hands-on
Meeting Magic 📅
45 minutes of meeting admin → 3 minutes with AI.
⏱ 15 minutes
Use a real JCI meeting. Think of a real meeting coming up — or use the facilitator's example on screen.

Before the meeting

Agenda generator
You are a coordinator at a youth leadership organization. Create a structured agenda for a 45-minute team meeting. Topic: [YOUR REAL TOPIC] Attendees: [NUMBER] members Main goals: [2–3 goals] Include: 4 agenda items, 1 key decision per item, suggested time allocation. Tone: clear and action-oriented.

After the meeting

Paste these rough notes into AI — then use the prompt below:

example_meeting_notes.txt Raw · unedited
ok meeting started late. Ahmed said event venue not confirmed yet. need answer before friday. Sara handles communication. Youssef contacts sponsors — 2 confirmed, 1 pending. Budget discussion — need 3000dh more. Next meeting — probably thursday but nobody confirmed.
Notes → action plan
Transform these raw meeting notes into a structured action plan. [PASTE YOUR NOTES HERE] Give me: 1. DECISIONS MADE 2. ACTION ITEMS — task | owner | deadline 3. URGENT FLAGS — things that can't wait 4. NEXT MEETING agenda (3 points) Format: clear, scannable, under 250 words.
Then try:
"Now write a WhatsApp follow-up message to share with the team." "Make it even shorter — just the urgent actions."

Activity 3 · Individual
Write Faster ✍️
Same message. 3 tones. 3 platforms. Under 3 minutes.
⏱ 10 minutes
Use this base text — or replace it with a real JCI message you need to write today.
source_message.txt Start here
JCI Tétouan is organizing a leadership training on March 20. Open to members and external participants. Registration until March 15. Fee: 80 DH.

3 versions · same message

📱 Instagram — engagement first ▾
Instagram version
Rewrite this for Instagram. [PASTE SOURCE TEXT] Rules: Strong hook (first line = what they gain). Enthusiastic tone. Emojis. Max 150 words. 5 relevant hashtags. End with a clear CTA.
💼 LinkedIn — professional & inspiring ▾
LinkedIn version
Rewrite this for LinkedIn. [PASTE SOURCE TEXT] Rules: Professional but warm tone. Opens with a question or bold statement about leadership. 3 short paragraphs. Ends with a question to drive comments. Max 220 words. No hashtag spam.
📲 WhatsApp — direct & friendly ▾
WhatsApp version
Rewrite this as a WhatsApp group message. [PASTE SOURCE TEXT] Rules: Friendly, like writing to a friend. Max 5 lines. Clear info (what, when, how much). End with one direct question. No hashtags. Easy to read on mobile.
Key insight: AI doesn't replace your message — it adapts it. You're still the author. AI is the editor.

Universal prompt — adapt for your format
You are a communication specialist for a youth leadership organization. Using this event information: [PASTE EVENT SOURCE HERE] Create a [YOUR FORMAT] for this event. Tone: [appropriate for your platform] Length: [your format's constraints] Goal: [what you want the reader to do]
Debrief: Each group reads their output aloud. Group votes on which version feels most natural for that platform. Discussion: what did AI get right? What did it miss?

Sprint structure

1
Choose your challenge & brief the AI
3 min
▾

Starter prompt structure
ROLE: You are a [role relevant to our challenge] CONTEXT: We are organizing INNOVACTION — a youth innovation competition in Tétouan, Morocco. JCI Tétouan event. [Add your specific situation: team size, current blockers, deadlines, budget, etc.] TASK: [Your specific task — be as concrete as possible] FORMAT: [What you need — length, tone, structure, language]
2
Review, discuss, and iterate
10 min
▾

Read the AI output together. Don't accept the first version — improve it as a team.

Stay in the same chat. Use follow-ups like: "Make it more urgent", "Add a specific deadline", "Give me 3 versions of the subject line".
Quick iteration prompts:
"What are we missing in this plan?" "Make the tone more professional." "Rewrite with a stronger opening." "Give me 3 different versions of this." "Add specific next steps with owners."

Prompt Library 📚
Ready-to-use prompts for common JCI tasks. Copy, customize, send.
Communication Sponsor outreach email ▾
A compelling first email to a potential sponsor — professional, specific, easy to reply to.
Prompt
You are a partnership manager at a youth NGO. Write a sponsorship outreach email to [COMPANY TYPE] for our event [EVENT NAME]. Context: JCI Tétouan · [event date] · [expected attendance] · [event goal] Include: Personalized opening, clear value proposition for the sponsor, 2–3 partnership tiers (brief), soft CTA to schedule a call. Tone: Professional but energetic. Length: Under 250 words. Language: [French/English].
Communication Partner thank-you email ▾
A warm, specific thank-you that strengthens the relationship for next time.
Prompt
Write a thank-you email to a sponsor/partner after our event. Event: [EVENT NAME] · Date: [DATE] · What went well: [2–3 highlights] Their contribution: [what they provided] Include: Specific mention of their impact, one memorable moment from the event, soft invitation to collaborate again. Tone: Warm, sincere, not generic. Length: 150–200 words.
Meetings Meeting agenda builder ▾
A structured, time-blocked agenda that keeps meetings focused.
Prompt
Create a [DURATION]-minute meeting agenda. Topic: [MAIN TOPIC] Attendees: [NUMBER] members — [ROLES if relevant] Goals: [2–3 outcomes you need] Format: numbered items, time per item, one key decision question per item, and a "parking lot" section for off-topic items. Action-oriented language.
Meetings Raw notes → action plan ▾
Transform messy meeting notes into a clean, shareable summary.
Prompt
Transform these raw meeting notes into a structured action plan. [PASTE NOTES HERE] Output format: ✅ DECISIONS MADE 📋 ACTION ITEMS — task | owner | deadline 🚨 URGENT FLAGS — things that can't wait 📅 NEXT MEETING — suggested agenda (3 points) Keep it scannable. Under 250 words. Don't add things not in the notes.
Content Event promotion — 3 platforms ▾
Generate Instagram, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp versions from one event brief.
Prompt
Create promotional content for this event across 3 platforms. Event: [NAME] · Date: [DATE] · Location: [PLACE] · Audience: [WHO] · Price: [COST] Key benefit for attendees: [ONE SENTENCE] Instagram: Engaging hook + emojis + 5 hashtags + CTA · Max 150 words LinkedIn: Professional storytelling + question to drive comments · Max 220 words WhatsApp: 5 lines max · friendly · direct invite · one question at end
Content 30-second verbal pitch ▾
A script you can actually say out loud — not read. For event introductions and pitches.
Prompt
Write a 30-second verbal pitch for [EVENT NAME]. Context: [2–3 key facts about the event] Audience: [who you're speaking to] Rules: Written as spoken language — not formal writing. Under 80 words. Opens with a hook (what the listener gains). One strong closing call to action. Natural rhythm — someone should be able to memorize it in 2 reads.
Organization Project timeline builder ▾
Turn a deadline and a goal into a realistic project timeline with milestones.
Prompt
Create a project timeline for [PROJECT NAME]. Start date: [DATE] · Event/deadline date: [DATE] Team: [NUMBER] volunteers, all part-time Key phases: [e.g., logistics, communication, recruitment, D-day] Output: Week-by-week plan with milestones, owners (use role names, not real names), and 3 key risk checkpoints. Table format.
Organization Weekly team update template ▾
A consistent weekly update your whole team can use — short enough that people actually send it.
Prompt
Design a weekly team update template for a volunteer team working on [PROJECT]. Requirements: Takes less than 3 minutes to fill out. Mobile-friendly. Covers: done this week, doing next week, blockers (1 max), mood check (emoji scale). Output the template AND one example of a filled-out version. Format: WhatsApp or message-friendly.
